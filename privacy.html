Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Google user data we access

When the channel operator connects a YouTube channel and grants permission, the app uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule videos, set thumbnails, add videos to playlists, and read the channel's basic information, scheduled videos and statistics.

How we use it

The data is used only to publish videos on the operator's own channel and to show the operator the publishing status and statistics of that channel. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties.

Storage

Access credentials and channel information are stored on the operator's private server and are used only for the purposes above. To request deletion, use the contact address below.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Contact

Questions and deletion requests: https://github.com/GuxPlus-02/youtube-app/issues
